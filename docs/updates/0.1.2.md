## SimPilot EFB 0.1.2

- Route waypoints now show their planned altitude under the name, colour-coded by phase (climb green, cruise cyan,
  descent orange), with **T/C** and **T/D** markers. Switch it off in NAV under "Waypoint altitudes, T/C, T/D".
- SimPilot Bridge on the PC is now a proper app: a window showing whether the iPad, MSFS and the chart panel are
  connected, a tray icon, an optional "Start with Windows" tick box and a **Check for updates** button.
  The old text-window version is still included as `SimPilot Bridge (console).cmd`.
- SimPilot Bridge on the PC can check for updates and install them: open `http://localhost:8386` in a browser on
  the PC and press **Check for updates**. This works from the next release onwards.
