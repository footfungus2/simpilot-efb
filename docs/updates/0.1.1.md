## SimPilot EFB 0.1.1

- Route waypoints now show their planned altitude under the name, colour-coded by phase (climb green, cruise cyan,
  descent orange), with **T/C** and **T/D** markers. Switch it off in NAV under "Waypoint altitudes, T/C, T/D".
- SimPilot Bridge on the PC is now a proper app (`SimPilot Bridge.exe`): a tray icon and a window in the iPad app's
  colours with Status, Charts, Log, Repair and Help pages. It shows whether the iPad, MSFS and the chart panel are
  connected, has an eye button to hide the PC's address, a "Start with Windows" switch, one-click firewall repair, a guided
  **Install chart panel** setup that checks MSFS 2024, the SDK and that the sim is closed before installing, and an
  **Install update** button. The old text-window version is still included as
  `SimPilot Bridge (console).cmd`.
- SimPilot Bridge on the PC can check for updates and install them: open `http://localhost:8386` in a browser on
  the PC and press **Check for updates**. This works from the next release onwards.
