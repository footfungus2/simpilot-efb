## SimPilot EFB 0.1.4

- Sim charts: SimPilot Link now shows each chart page and SimPilot Bridge copies it from the screen. MSFS blocks reading
  chart images inside the sim, and the old canvas readback could crash it. Keep the panel open and visible while
  charts download; a bigger or popped-out panel is faster and sharper.
- Fenix A320 controls no longer get stuck on "waiting for simulator readback" after the aircraft loads.
- The SimPilot Link installer waits for MSFS to close instead of starting it twice.

Windows/MSFS capture and physical iPad checks remain pending.
