## SimPilot EFB 0.1.4

- SimPilot Link shows startup, build identity, target and last-request details. Bridge identity is checked without
  sending a work poll, and HTTP or protocol errors retain their explanation.
- **Repair SimPilot Link.bat** now updates and hash-checks the panel HTML, CSS and script together, retaining a backup.
  **Diagnose SimPilot Link.bat** collects local package and connection evidence without marking the panel connected.
- Your flight's charts are exported ahead of time: when a SimBrief flight is loaded (or the PC bridge connects), the SID,
  and at the destination the STAR and the approaches for the planned runway, are exported in the background while the
  SimPilot Link panel is open. A chart you open yourself always goes first.
- Sim chart capture is sharper and safer: pages are captured at their own resolution (one screen pixel per chart pixel),
  every tile is checked against a tile number shown by the panel so a half-drawn tile is never stitched in, it copes with
  mixed-DPI displays and washed-out (HDR) colours, and errors say whether the panel is hidden, cut off or too small.
  Update SimPilot Bridge and SimPilot Link together (link protocol 3).
- Sim charts: SimPilot Link now shows each chart page and SimPilot Bridge copies it from the screen. MSFS blocks reading
  chart images inside the sim, and the old canvas readback could crash it. Keep the panel open and visible while
  charts download; a bigger or popped-out panel is faster and sharper.
- Sim charts open faster: an airport's chart list appears on the iPad at once, and a chart is exported from the sim the
  first time you open it (a few seconds) instead of the whole airport up front. Loading a flight no longer queues every
  chart of its airports. Exports carry on where they stopped, a lost panel is retried, **Export again from the sim**
  redoes a page that came out wrong, and the chart list refreshes from the sim once a day while the panel is open.
  Update SimPilot Link too: run **Install SimPilot Link** again with MSFS closed.
- SimPilot Bridge now uses port 8386 instead of 8380. FlyByWire SimBridge also uses 8380, and while it ran the
  SimPilot Link panel couldn't find SimPilot Bridge. The panel now says what is wrong: SimPilot Bridge not started, or
  another program on its port. Update SimPilot Bridge and SimPilot Link together. A bridge address saved in the app
  with :8380 switches to :8386 on its own.
- SimPilot Link handles HTTP replies delivered through ready-state changes and retries requests that stay silent,
  so one stalled request no longer leaves the panel stuck on "Bridge searching…".
- Installed panels can receive the repair through **Repair SimPilot Link.bat** with MSFS closed.
  It keeps the compiled panel and a backup of the old installation, avoiding another SDK build.
- Fenix A320 controls no longer get stuck on "waiting for simulator readback" after the aircraft loads.
- The SimPilot Link installer waits for MSFS to close instead of starting it twice.

Windows/MSFS capture, and physical iPad checks remain pending.
