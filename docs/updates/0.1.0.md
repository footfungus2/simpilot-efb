## SimPilot EFB 0.1.0

First release.

- Enroute map with globe view, route, airports, FIRs, navaids and weather layers (METAR, TAF, SIGMET), plus an airport
  moving map with taxiways, runways, stands and an ON RWY / APPROACHING RWY banner.
- Flight Folder with SimBrief flight plans, route and flight progress, vertical profile and a weather briefing.
- VATSIM layer in the style of VATSIM Radar (ATC with real sector ownership, approach areas, traffic counts) and a
  VATSIM assistant for your own flight plan.
- Charts from the sim's built-in Lido provider, exported through the SimPilot Link toolbar panel in MSFS 2024:
  the airport's chart list appears at once, each chart is exported the first time you open it and then kept on the PC
  and the iPad. Your flight's SID, STAR and approaches for the planned runway are exported ahead of time while the
  panel is open. Pages are captured at their own resolution, and a bigger panel exports faster.
- Your aircraft drawn on georeferenced charts with follow mode, a chart overlaid on the map, Apple Pencil annotations,
  split view and a saved **★ FLIGHT** chart pack that works without the PC.
- A **Flight** screen: departure and arrival briefing with live QNH, wind and the VATSIM ATIS, an ATC clearance pad with
  Pencil notes, editable checklists with per-flight progress, fuel against the SimBrief plan, a logbook that records
  takeoffs and landings by itself, and your own PDFs with bookmarks and Pencil notes.
- **Edit Flight** sets the runway and SID/STAR in use, and the charts follow it.
- Fenix A320 controls (radios, transponder, FCU, lights) through the PC bridge.
- Optional update checks and local diagnostics.
- SimPilot Bridge for the PC (no Node.js installation needed) and SimPilot Link panel installer, repair and diagnose tools.

Live Windows/MSFS chart capture and physical iPad checks are still being validated; see `docs/release-validation.md`.
